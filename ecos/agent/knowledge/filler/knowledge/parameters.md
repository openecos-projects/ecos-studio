## parameter.filler.workspace_config

**Meaning:** The Filler workspace-configuration boundary.

**Role:** The runner passes it to `run_filler` before saving the post-insertion database.

**Source evidence:** **ecc.runner**, **ecc.module**

<a id="parameter.filler.cell_selection"></a>
## parameter.filler.cell_selection

**Meaning:** The filler-cell selection supplied by the workspace configuration.

**Role:** It constrains which physical filler cells the underlying tool may insert.

**Source evidence:** **ecc.runner**, **ecc.module**
