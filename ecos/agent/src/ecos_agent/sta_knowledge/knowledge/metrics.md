## metric.sta_setup_wns

**Meaning:** `sta_setup_wns` is the normalized sta metric shown by ECOS when its source fact is available.

**Calculation:** ECC reads all available corner QoR summaries, selects the worst setup/hold WNS and TNS and lowest frequency, records violation and coverage counts, then emits signoff facts and timing-issue artifacts. Missing source data remains unavailable; it is not converted to zero or a success claim.

**Source evidence:** **ecc.runner**, **ecc.module**, **ecc.metrics**, **gui.step_metrics**
