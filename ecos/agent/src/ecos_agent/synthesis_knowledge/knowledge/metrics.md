## metric.synthesis_cell_area

**Meaning:** `synthesis_cell_area` is the normalized Synthesis metric shown by ECOS when its source fact is available.

**Calculation:** Yosys reads its stat JSON and publishes cell count, cell area, wire count, and port count; available post-synthesis power facts are added separately. Missing source data remains unavailable; it is not converted to zero or a success claim.

**Source evidence:** **yosys.runner**, **yosys.metrics**, **ecc.runner**, **ecc.metrics**, **gui.step_metrics**
