## metric.fanout_max

**Meaning:** `fanout_max` is the normalized fixFanout metric shown by ECOS when its source fact is available.

**Calculation:** ECC publishes maximum fanout together with database instance and net counts after net optimization. Missing source data remains unavailable; it is not converted to zero or a success claim.

**Source evidence:** **ecc.runner**, **ecc.module**, **ecc.metrics**, **gui.step_metrics**
