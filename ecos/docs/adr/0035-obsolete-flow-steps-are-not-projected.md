# Obsolete Flow Steps are not projected into the current product

When a Flow Step is removed from ECC and ECOS Studio, legacy records and Artifacts for that step are ignored during migration and projection. The obsolete step is not displayed, executed, rerun, used as a branch boundary, included in comparisons or allowed to affect current Flow outcomes; preserving historical visibility would keep removed product semantics alive across every consumer.
